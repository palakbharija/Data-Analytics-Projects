# Task 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Business Intelligence

## 📌 Overview

This task focuses on exploring a cleaned sales dataset to identify important patterns, trends, relationships, and business insights. The analysis was performed using Python, SQL, and Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), answer business questions using SQL, create meaningful visualizations, and develop a static sales dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform descriptive and exploratory analysis of the sales dataset.
- Identify patterns and trends across products, categories, cities, age groups, and months.
- Use SQL to answer specific business questions.
- Analyze relationships between different variables.
- Create meaningful data visualizations.
- Build a static dashboard highlighting important business KPIs and insights.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** – Data analysis and visualization
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization
- **SQL / MySQL** – Business-oriented data analysis
- **Microsoft Excel** – Pivot tables, charts, and dashboard creation
- **Jupyter Notebook** – EDA implementation

---

## 📊 Dataset

The cleaned dataset contains **1,000 sales transactions** and **14 columns**.

Key fields include:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Age
- Gender
- City
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Sales
- Month
- Age Group

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Descriptive & Exploratory Analysis

The dataset was analyzed to understand sales distributions and customer patterns using summary statistics and visualizations.

### 2. SQL Business Analysis

Business questions were formulated and answered using SQL queries involving:

- Filtering
- Aggregation
- Grouping
- Sorting
- Ranking
- Business-oriented sales analysis

### 3. Data Visualization

Visualizations were created to analyze:

- Total Sales by Category
- Total Sales by Age Group
- Total Sales by City
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Monthly Sales Trend

### 4. Dashboard

A static Sales & Business Insights Dashboard was created in Excel containing key performance indicators:

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Average Sales per Order
- Total Transactions

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Electronics** generated the highest sales among the product categories.
- The **Middle Age** customer group contributed the highest total sales.
- **Patna** recorded the highest total sales among the analyzed cities.
- **Laptop and Mobile** were among the leading products by sales.
- Monthly sales showed noticeable fluctuations throughout the year, highlighting changing sales performance over time.
